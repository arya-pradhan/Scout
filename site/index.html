How it works

Photon carries the conversation, TinyFish does the web work, Claude decides what to do and writes the reply.

📱 iMessage⇄ Photon Spectrum⇄ Scout + Claude (18 tools)→ TinyFish Search · Fetch · Agent→ the live web

Photon Spectrum

The iMessage layer

  • A real iMessage number: every message in and out
  • Texts first: briefs, nudges, alerts, reminders
  • Tapback reactions as controls
  • Typing indicator, message effects (🎉 🎆), rich links, contact card

TinyFish

The web layer, with all three endpoints doing real work

  • Search: discovers course sites, events pages, fresh postings
  • Fetch: crawls professor sites, events pages and job boards
  • Agent: verifies postings and reads application forms in a real browser

Claude Sonnet 5.5

The brain

  • Understands free-form texts and picks the right tools
  • Turns messy pages into dated deadlines and events
  • Only states facts that came back from a tool, with links

Every TinyFish endpoint, and why it's there

🔎 Search

"Find it" for a course's website or your school's events page · a job-search watch using recent results only · research answers with sources.

Scout doesn't have a URL yet, or the postings live across the whole web.

📄 Fetch

Crawls a professor's site: the page plus its linked Calendar / Deliverables / Syllabus pages and PDFs, batched · events pages at schools without an API · job boards.

These pages are messy, JS-rendered or PDFs. Fetch returns clean text and links.

🤖 Agent

Confirms a posting is still open before Scout texts you · reads real application forms (questions, documents, deadline) · browses JS-heavy course sites and boards.

Some answers take clicking through a live site. Every goal is read-only.

Works at any school

Onboarding asks for links, and Scout figures out how to read them.

🎓Your school sets your timezone: every time Scout shows is local to you.
📚Canvas via your private read-only calendar feed. No password needed.
🧑‍🏫Any professor website: HTML, schedule tables, PDFs, "EOW" deadlines.
🎉Campus Labs Engage and Localist calendars, or any events page.
💼Any field: web-search watch, any job board, Greenhouse / Lever / Ashby careers pages, plus the Simplify list for tech.

Built to be trusted

A friend who texts you useful things, not a bot that does things behind your back.

Read-only on the web

Agent goals forbid signing in, typing into forms, applying or submitting. Scout can't book, buy, RSVP or email anything.

You approve every add

Calendar links open pre-filled; you tap Save. Essay drafts only when you say yes.

Never fakes a source

Deadlines, events and postings come only from real pages, each with its link. Items without a real date are dropped.

Quiet by default

Deduped alerts, quiet hours, and no brief on days with nothing to say. Failures are named, not hidden.
